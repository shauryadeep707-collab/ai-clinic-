Popular lookups:

Paracetamol (Acetaminophen)

Brand Names: Crocin, Calpol, Dolo 650
Over-the-Counter (OTC)
Common Uses:

Mild-to-moderate pain relief, fever reduction.

Dosage Forms:

Tablet (500mg, 650mg), Syrup

Precautions & Daily Limits:

Do not exceed 4,000 mg in 24 hours. Avoid concurrent alcohol use.

Contraindications:

Severe hepatic impairment or active liver disease.

Potential Side Effects:

Nausea, rash (rare), hepatic toxicity with overdose.

Source Reference:

British National Formulary (BNF); WHO Model List of Essential Medicines.
