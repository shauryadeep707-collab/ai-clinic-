How It Works

Follow our 4-step seamless process for medical record organization, AI assistance, and doctor consultations.

1

Upload Your Medical Reports Privately

Securely upload PDFs, scans, or images of lab reports, imaging, or prescriptions. Our processing pipeline parses text, extracts numerical lab parameters (e.g. Hemoglobin, Glucose, Vitamin D), and never overwrites past records.

2

Receive Grounded AI Explanations

The AI engine generates plain-English summaries grounded strictly in the test reference ranges. You receive key takeaways, normal/abnormal highlights, and practical questions to discuss with your doctor.

3

Unified Chronological Health Timeline

Every uploaded report, symptom consultation, doctor appointment, and prescription is automatically synchronized into your Unified Health Timeline, providing a complete lifelong longitudinal health journey.

4

Consult 100% Verified Doctors

Connect with certified clinicians across cardiology, dermatology, and general medicine for video or in-person consultations. Share authorized records with a single click.
