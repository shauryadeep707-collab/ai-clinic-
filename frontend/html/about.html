About AI Health Clinic

AI Health Clinic is an advanced healthcare technology platform designed to bridge the gap between complex medical documents, evidence-based triage guidance, and licensed clinical practitioners.

Our Core Mission

Healthcare documents and lab tests are often filled with clinical terminology that causes unnecessary anxiety or misunderstanding. Our goal is to empower patients with transparent, grounded, easy-to-understand explanations of their medical records while maintaining rigorous safety standards.

We strictly separate AI educational triage from licensed clinical diagnosis. Our system never independently prescribes pharmaceuticals, fabricates interaction data, or replaces a professional doctor.

Data Isolation & Privacy

Built with strict patient record isolation. Doctors only access records when explicitly authorized by the patient.

Verified Medical Faculty

Multi-stage qualification checks with DigiLocker NAD repository and State Medical Council verification workflows.

Clinical Grounding

Independent safety-rule layers for emergency signs. AI explanations cite peer-reviewed NHS and Mayo Clinic sources.
