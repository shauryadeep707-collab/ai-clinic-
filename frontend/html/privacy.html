Privacy Policy & Data Security

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Data Isolation & Patient Ownership

All health data, medical records, consultations, and test documents are owned exclusively by the patient. Under our multi-tenant authorization model, no other patient or unauthorized clinician can access your records by modifying IDs in request URLs or parameters.

2. Encryption in Transit and at Rest

User passwords are encrypted using one-way salt hashing (bcrypt). All API communications occur over HTTPS (TLS 1.3). Medical document files are kept in private server directories not accessible by public web URLs.

3. External Pharmacy Data Privacy

When using our Pharmacy Connect feature, AI Health Clinic does NOT transmit your medical history, confidential symptoms, or patient ID to external pharmacy providers (Amazon Pharmacy, Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart, Tata 1mg, Apollo Pharmacy, PharmEasy, Netmeds). Links open public search or product landing pages only.

4. Doctor Record Sharing Consent

Doctors cannot access patient reports or timelines unless the patient books an appointment or explicitly grants record sharing consent in the Settings portal. Consent can be revoked at any time.
