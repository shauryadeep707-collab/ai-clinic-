# AI Medicine Guidance & Clinical Safety Policy

## Clinical Principles
1. **Safety Before Medicine Information**: The AI evaluates red-flag symptoms (severe chest pain, breathing difficulties, stroke symptoms) before presenting medicine education.
2. **No Independent Prescribing**: The AI assistant never prescribes antibiotics or prescription-only medicines (Schedule H/H1 in India).
3. **No Dose Alterations**: The AI never changes, increases, or stops clinician-prescribed dosages.
4. **Allergy Cross-Referencing**: Searches are cross-referenced with the patient's documented medical allergies (e.g. penicillin allergy warning for Amoxicillin).
5. **Verified Sources**: All indications, precautions, and contraindications cite established references (British National Formulary, WHO Essential Medicines, National Formulary of India).
